Ducks are fascinating waterfowl known for their distinctive waddling gait and quacking sounds. They belong to the family Anatidae and are found in both freshwater and saltwater habitats around the world. Ducks have a diverse diet, feeding on aquatic plants, insects, and small fish. Their waterproof feathers, thanks to a special oil they produce, allow them to stay dry while swimming. Social creatures, ducks often form flocks and are known for their migratory patterns, traveling long distances between breeding and wintering grounds. With their vibrant plumage and playful behavior, ducks are a delight to observe in nature.
